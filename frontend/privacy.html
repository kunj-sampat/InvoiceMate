Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

At InvoiceMate ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the confidential financial, client, and personal information you entrust to our invoicing and billing operating system.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account or use InvoiceMate, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Your full name, work email address, hashed password, and organization name.
  • Third-Party Single Sign-On (OAuth): When signing in via Google or LinkedIn, we receive basic verified identity data (full name, email address, and profile photo avatar) in accordance with OAuth 2.0 standards.
  • Business & Billing Records: Client contact details, line items, invoice numbers, tax rates (GST/HSN), discount figures, and expense items that you create to generate bills and track profitability.
  • Payment Identifiers: Virtual Payment Addresses (UPI VPAs), payment modes, and payment receipt confirmations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to deliver and maintain high-grade invoicing services:

  • To authenticate your identity and safeguard your workspace sessions via signed JWT tokens.
  • To render PDF invoices, generate scan-to-pay dynamic UPI QR codes, and prepare client statements.
  • To calculate net revenue, categorize business expenses, and produce GSTR-1 compliant tax summaries.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your customer lists or billing entries to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your records are persisted on secure, enterprise-grade cloud database infrastructure with end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Passwords are never stored in plaintext and are securely hashed using industry-standard salted BCrypt cryptography.

4. Third-Party Integrations

InvoiceMate interfaces with authorized third-party identity and infrastructure providers:

  • Google Identity Services: Used strictly for secure authentication upon your explicit user consent.
  • LinkedIn OpenID Connect: Used strictly for profile verification and authentication.

5. Your Rights & Data Ownership

You retain 100% full ownership over your invoicing and client data. You may download, export (via CSV or PDF), update, or delete your client records and invoices at any time from your account workspace.
