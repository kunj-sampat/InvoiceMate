Powerful Features

Built Specifically for Modern Indian Businesses

Everything you need to bill clients cleanly, collect payments on time, manage inventory, and file GST without headaches.

Dynamic UPI QR Code

Every invoice features an automatic UPI QR code pre-coded with your UPI ID and invoice amount. Clients scan via GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm for instant payment.

1-Click WhatsApp Invoices

Directly share professional invoice links and polite overdue reminders straight to your customer's WhatsApp with a single tap. No messy manual attachments.

GST & HSN/SAC Directory

Auto-fill HSN/SAC codes and automated CGST, SGST, or IGST tax splits. Export 1-click quarterly GSTR-1 summaries formatted directly for your CA.

Recurring Invoices & Retainers

Effortlessly manage monthly retainers, software subscriptions, and maintenance contracts. Automate repeat billing with 1-click generation.

Customer Ledgers & Statements

Provide transparent client statements with complete date-wise transaction history, debits, credits, running balance, and instant PDF/Print export.

Product Catalog & Inventory

Maintain your product catalog, auto-deduct stock quantities upon invoice dispatch, and get timely warnings before running out of inventory.
